Product Care Guide

Our slippers are handcrafted using premium materials designed to last. With proper care, they will age beautifully and stay comfortable for years. Follow these simple tips to keep them looking their best.

1

Leather Care

  • check_circle Clean regularly: Wipe off dust and dirt with a soft, dry cloth. For deeper cleaning, use a slightly damp cloth but avoid soaking the leather.
  • check_circle Condition: Apply a high-quality leather conditioner every 3-6 months to keep the leather supple and prevent cracking.
  • check_circle Avoid water: Leather and water don't mix well. If they get wet, let them air dry naturally away from direct heat sources like radiators or sunlight.
2

Fabric & Kente Care

  • check_circle Spot clean: Use a mild detergent mixed with water and a soft brush (like a toothbrush) to gently scrub stains on fabric areas.
  • check_circle Protect: You can use a fabric protector spray to repel water and stains, especially for light-colored fabrics.
  • cancel Do not machine wash: The structure of the slipper may be damaged in a washing machine. Hand cleaning is always best.
3

Storage

When not in use, store your slippers in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight, which can fade colors and dry out leather. Using a dust bag is recommended for long-term storage.
