Return & Exchange Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Our Guarantee

We want you to love your slippers! If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, we're here to help. You can return or exchange items within 14 days of delivery.

Conditions for Returns

To be eligible for a return, your item must be:

  • Unused and in the same condition that you received it.
  • In the original packaging.
  • Accompanied by the receipt or proof of purchase.

Note: Custom-made or personalized items cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or defective.

Exchanges

If you need to exchange an item for a different size or color, the fastest way is to return the original item and place a new order. Alternatively, contact our support team, and we can guide you through a direct exchange process.

Refunds

Once we receive your return, we will inspect it and notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. If approved, your refund will be processed, and a credit will automatically be applied to your original method of payment (e.g., Mobile Money reversal) within 5-7 business days.

Shipping Costs

You will be responsible for paying for your own shipping costs for returning your item, unless the return is due to our error (e.g., wrong item sent, defective product). Shipping costs are non-refundable.

How to initiate a return:

Please contact our support team on WhatsApp with your Order ID and reason for return.
